# Decide whether internal/sink follows the R14 package home

- raised: 2026-11-04 — standards audit P3 of plan 261029.0012 (R14), deferred by the owner
- decision: open

R14 places shared utilities under `internal/tools/<name>` and shared HTTP plumbing under `internal/tools/rest/<name>`. The restructure created `internal/sink` (and `internal/httpx`, see its own entry) directly under `internal`. Decide whether this library keeps the flat layout as a recorded deviation or moves the packages to the R14 homes.
