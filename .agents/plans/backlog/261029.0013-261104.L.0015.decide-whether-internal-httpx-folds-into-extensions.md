# Decide whether internal/httpx folds into extensions

- raised: 2026-11-04 — standards audit P3 of plan 261029.0013 (R9/R14), deferred by the owner
- decision: open

`internal/httpx` was created so `httptap` and `extensions` could share the response-writer composition and path escaping. After `httptap` was removed, `extensions` is its only importer. Folding it into `extensions` as unexported code would remove a package and the exported methods on its unexported types; keeping it keeps a deliberate boundary. No behaviour changes either way.
