# Stream-decode Axiom responses over 1 MiB

- raised: 2026-10-28 — deferred by plan 261021.0006 (round-3 review, P3)
- decision: open

`deliver` reads an HTTP 200 body through a 1 MiB `LimitReader` and decodes it with `json.Unmarshal`. With `WithBatchSize(10000)` and a fully failed batch, the `failures` array can exceed 1 MiB. The truncated body then fails to decode, and the whole batch counts as Failed, including any events that were ingested. Proposal: walk the body with a `json.Decoder` token stream, read `ingested` and `failed`, keep the first `failures[].error`, and skip the rest of the array. Reachable only with batch sizes well above the default of 1000.
