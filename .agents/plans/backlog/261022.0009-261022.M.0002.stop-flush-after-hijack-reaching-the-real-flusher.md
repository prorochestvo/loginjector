# Stop Flush after Hijack reaching the real flusher

- raised: 2026-10-22 — standards audit of plan 261022.0009 (httptap hardening), deferred as Worth a look
- decision: open

After a downstream handler hijacks the connection, `Flush` on the httptap composite writers is still forwarded to the underlying `http.Flusher`; `beforeFlush` is a no-op but the real `Flush` runs. Decide whether the composite `Flush` should return early once the writer is marked hijacked (net/http reports `ErrHijacked` through `FlushError`), and add a hijack-then-Flush subtest that asserts the real flusher is not called.
