# Stop Flush after Hijack reaching the real flusher

- raised: 2026-10-22 — standards audit of plan 261022.0009 (httptap hardening), deferred as Worth a look
- groomed: 2026-11-15 16:49Z
- decision: open

After a downstream handler hijacks the connection, `Flush` on the composite writers in internal/httpx/rw.go (`flusherRW`, `flusherHijackerRW`, `flusherPusherRW`, `allThreeRW`) is still forwarded to the underlying `http.Flusher`; `BeforeFlush` runs but the real `Flush` follows unconditionally. Decide whether the composite `Flush` should return early once the writer is marked hijacked (net/http reports `ErrHijacked` through `FlushError`), and add a hijack-then-Flush subtest that asserts the real flusher is not called. The post-handler flush guard in extensions/http_payload.go already skips its own flush after a hijack; this entry concerns a Flush issued by the wrapped handler itself.

Rewritten 2026-11-15 (grooming): the composite writers moved from httptap to internal/httpx (plans 261029.0012 and 261029.0013); the behaviour is unchanged.
